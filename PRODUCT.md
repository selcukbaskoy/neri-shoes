# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two distinct user types sharing the same surface:

1. **Wholesale buyers** (esnaf, küçük mağaza sahipleri, butik işletmeciler): Visit to assess product range and minimum quantities. They are pragmatic; they need to trust the manufacturer before picking up the phone. Context: desktop, office or shop floor, business hours.

2. **Retail end customers** (erkek, 25–55, orta-üst segment): Discovering the brand via referral or search. They want to feel the premium quality through the screen before committing. Context: mobile, browsing casually, may switch to WhatsApp to ask questions.

Both audiences respond to craftsmanship signals over lifestyle photography. The brand earns trust through material honesty, not aspirational imagery.

## Product Purpose

Neri Shoes is an Adana-based manufacturer and seller of men's leather shoes. The website serves as the primary digital storefront and wholesale inquiry gateway. Success means a visitor either adds a product to their WhatsApp inquiry, books a wholesale conversation, or remembers the brand name next time they need leather shoes.

## Brand Personality

**Ustanın işi. Sessiz özgüven. Deri kokusu.**

Three words: **Crafted · Quiet · Enduring**

Emotional goal: the site should feel like walking into a well-lit leather workshop — warm, expert, unhurried. Not a luxury fashion house, not a fast-fashion retailer. A craftsman who has been making the same shoe, better, for decades.

## Anti-references

- **Mass-market shoe brands** (Nike, Adidas web aesthetic): sport, energy, hero video loops — opposite register entirely.
- **Generic luxury e-commerce** (cream background, thin sans-serif, aspirational models): the warm-neutral parchment trap; what AI generates by default for "premium leather."
- **Corporate B2B catalogues**: cold, dense, no soul.
- **Glassmorphism / gradient-heavy SaaS**: decorative tech aesthetics have no place here.
- **Eyebrow-on-every-section scaffold** (01 · About, 02 · Products): the AI grammar tell; this brand speaks directly, without scaffolding.

## Design Principles

1. **Zanaat konuşur, ürün öne çıkar.** Let the shoe be the hero. Decorative UI elements should recede; the product image and the material quality should lead.
2. **Sıcak sessizlik.** Whitespace and restraint create luxury here, not gloss. Dense, animated, or visually noisy layouts undercut the craftsman feel.
3. **İki müşteriye tek ses.** Toptan alıcı da perakende müşteri de aynı sayfayı görür. The design must feel trustworthy to the pragmatist and beautiful to the aspirant simultaneously.
4. **Altın, vurgu — zemin değil.** Gold (#FFD000) is an accent, never a background fill. Use it for dividers, hover states, borders, and focal points only.
5. **Bölgesel özgünlük.** Adana / Türkiye craft heritage is a differentiator, not a liability. The brand voice can acknowledge it without becoming folksy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum. Body text must hit 4.5:1 on the black (#000) background.
- RTL layout already implemented for Arabic (ar) locale — preserve all RTL utilities.
- Multi-language: tr (base), en, de, it, ar, ru — all translations must be complete before shipping any new surface.
- Reduce-motion: every animation must have a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.
